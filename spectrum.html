Does the measured wiring have a spectrum random wiring lacks?

Eigenvalues of the weight matrix, measured against randomisations that each preserve something different. This is the structural version of the question the task experiments kept answering with "no".

Read the normalisation before the numbers. The lab's default makes each cell's absolute inputs sum to one, which bounds the spectral radius at 1 by construction, so a measured value near 1 means the matrix nearly attains its bound rather than that the fly sits at the edge of chaos. Two other normalisations are offered, and the answer changes between them. Subset selection, the log compression of synapse counts and the sign assignment are all lab choices, so everything here describes the lab's matrices, not the fly.
