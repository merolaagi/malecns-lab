Inspect the assumptions

What comes from the specimen?

Neuron IDs, annotations, directed edges, synapse counts and neurotransmitter predictions. The loaded subset and original file checksums are downloadable.

What did we choose?

1 ms LIF updates; 20 ms membrane and 10 ms synapse time constants; log-transformed, normalized weights; acetylcholine positive, GABA and glutamate negative; unresolved transmitters contribute zero. Transmitter signs are approximations, not measured receptor effects.

Six engineered phase oscillators turn motor rates into movement. Sensory cells receive a hand-designed stance signal. Target mode injects target bearing directly into descending cells; it does not model smell or vision.

What would establish an advance?

Fit on biological recordings, then predict held-out neural perturbations. Replace pooled motor readouts with verified muscle mappings and mechanics. Current ablations are software experiments only.
