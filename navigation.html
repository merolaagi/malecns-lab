Finding the way home

The fly's navigation algorithm as a model: a ring of heading cells holds a compass, rotation cells turn it, vector cells accumulate the path travelled, and steering cells compare a goal against heading and push the descending neurons. The fly wanders, then tries to get back.

A published algorithm, not a measurement. The roles of these cell types come from the navigation literature; the ring dynamics, gains, drift and movement model are ours. With idealised connectivity the ring offsets are the published ones. With measured connectivity they come from data/central-complex.json, built by build_centralcomplex.py from the raw tables; without that file the measured option reports what to run. Whether the fly computes it this way is not tested here.
