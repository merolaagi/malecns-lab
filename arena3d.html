Three flies in 3D

The same arena run, drawn as jointed 3D bodies. Every leg joint is posed by the motor neurons of its own muscles; the parts the connectome subset cannot drive are yours to move by hand.

Brain-driven and hand-driven parts are labelled separately. Coxa, trochanter and tibia joints on all six legs follow their measured motor neurons, and the tarsus follows them on the front legs only, because tarsus levator motor neurons are annotated only there. Head, antennae, wings and abdomen have no motor neurons in this subset, so they move only when you move them. A fly's compound eyes cannot move at all.
