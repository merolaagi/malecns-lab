Does it matter where inhibition sits?

The measured network is less excitable than matched random wiring, and moving which cells are inhibitory changes that a lot, from only about 6% of cells. This asks whether the placement also changes what the network can compute.

Fixed wiring, trained readout. The measured graph is used as a recurrent network and only a linear readout is fitted, the standard reservoir test. Conditions keep the number of inhibitory cells identical and move only which cells they are. Measured: the graph and the transmitter predictions. Assumed: the dynamics, scaling, readout and tasks, and that a transmitter prediction fixes a cell's sign.
