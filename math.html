Read the result carefully

Memorization versus transfer

Perfect answers on taught examples can be memorization. Unseen pairs test limited generalization within 0–9. Even success here would not establish an addition algorithm or out-of-range arithmetic.

What we supplied

Each digit is a random synthetic PN activation pattern. The two operand slots use separate input channels. Measured PN → KC weights generate sparse features. MBONs are randomly assigned to 19 answer pools.

Supervised cross-entropy updates shared gains on existing KC → MBON edges using an anatomical PAM eligibility gate. Digit encoding, output labels, update rule, and positive weight interpretation are engineering assumptions.

Controls and leakage checks

Neither the correct sum nor a precomputed sum feature enters the encoder or predictor. Labels enter only teaching and scoring. Withheld questions are paired with their reversals. No-learning, shuffled-label, shuffled-wiring, and KC-silenced controls use the same split.
