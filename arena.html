Three flies, one arena

Two agents labelled male and one labelled female, each running the same measured locomotion circuit, coupled by hand-designed senses: odour, sight of each other, and a courtship song signal.

One connectome, three copies, engineered senses. MaleCNS is a single male fly, and the leg-circuit subset contains none of the sexually dimorphic courtship circuitry, so there is no female brain to run. Every agent here runs the identical measured circuit; "female" labels what an agent emits and how it responds, both of which are parameters chosen by us. Sensing, signalling and the drive rules are engineered, so this shows how three copies of one measured circuit behave when coupled by assumed senses, not how flies behave.

Each fly is drawn to scale (2.5 mm body). Every leg is posed by its own joint angles, each driven by the motor neurons innervating that joint's muscles, and a planted foot carries a dot. One wing extends while a male sings. A fly's compound eyes cannot move, and the antennal and neck motor neurons are not in this subset, so head, eyes and antennae stay fixed. The faint haloes show the odour field the agents sense.

What happened

Leg joints

Each joint angle is the difference in firing rate between the motor neurons of its own muscles: coxa protractors against retractors, trochanter levator against depressors, tibia extensor against flexors, tarsus levator against depressors. Teal means agonist-dominant, gold antagonist-dominant, and ▪ marks a planted leg. Tarsus levator motor neurons exist only for the front legs, so the other four tarsi cannot be driven.

Measured and engineered

Measured: each agent's 807-cell locomotion circuit, its dynamics, and the transmitter signs.

Engineered: odour falling off with distance, two-antenna comparison, seeing another fly as a bearing and apparent size, who is attracted to whom, the song rule, and the drive levels. Below about 0.7 of the default drive the circuit produces no motor output, so baseline exploration sits just above that.

There is no collision physics, so agents can overlap.
