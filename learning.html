Where the behavior comes from

Measured structure

Only PN → KC, KC → MBON and PAM → KC connections are retained. Both hemispheres are included. Recurrent pathways and much of the brain are omitted.

Supplied learning mechanism

Two synthetic patterns stimulate projection neurons. A top-5% activity threshold sparsifies Kenyon-cell responses. Reward prediction error updates gains on existing KC → MBON edges, gated by PAM → KC contact strength.

All MBON outputs are pooled into an assumed reward-value readout relative to the initial weights. Biological approach/avoidance signs and compartment-specific dopamine dynamics are not recovered here.

Memory and unbiased tests

Probe trials deliver no reward and make no weight updates. Left/right locations are counterbalanced. Testing uses noisy odor exemplars distinct from training. A declared decay multiplier is applied once before the delayed-memory block; abstract steps are not seconds.

How to interpret success

Learning with this rule is a constructed capability. Shuffled networks may succeed equally well. Passing this task does not demonstrate general intelligence, biological fidelity, or superiority of the measured connectome.
